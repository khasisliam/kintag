Someone Needs Help

The red tag you just scanned contains an emergency contact phone number.

📱 + 🔴
(Optional: You can replace this box with a GIF or photo of someone tapping the tag with their phone)
1
Unlock your smartphone.
The scanner will not work if your screen is off.
Android Users: Make sure NFC is ON
iPhone Users: Your NFC is always on automatically! in your settings.
2
Hold the very top of your phone directly against the red tag.
Keep it there for 2-3 seconds.
3
Tap the notification.
A link will pop up on your screen. Tap it to initiate the phone call immediately.
